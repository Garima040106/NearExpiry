What NearExpiry offers

One platform. Better inventory decisions.

Tools to help facilities keep track of medicines and coordinate available stock.

◷

Expiry tracking

Monitor medicine batches and identify expired or soon-to-expire stock through inventory status indicators.

▤

Inventory management

Maintain medicine batch records, update stock quantities and manage inventory at your healthcare facility.

⇄

Surplus sharing

List eligible surplus medicines so that other registered facilities can discover and request available stock.

✓

Request management

Create medicine requests and allow authorized pharmacists to approve or reject requests with recorded decisions.

⌕

Audit records

Maintain a history of medicine-sharing activities, including request creation, approval and rejection.

⚙

Role-based access

Separate staff, pharmacist and administrator permissions to support controlled access to system operations.

How NearExpiry works
